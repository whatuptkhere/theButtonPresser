These are the 3D files for the buttonPresser project.

They are designed around a standard-sized servo and a 30mm threaded arcade button. Note that the button does not thread into the print, it's intended to simply be glued to ensure it works as well as possible on whatever printer you're using.

You may need to print these with lots of support, or you may not. It really depends on your printer. They're pretty easy to squirt out on a Bambu Labs P2S.
